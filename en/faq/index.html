What should I do when I hit "Error: quota exceeded"?

That message means your current 5-hour rolling window (or weekly Free cap) is exhausted. Immediately run /compact, switch small edits to Fast mode, or wait for the 5-hour window to roll over. If you hit walls more than twice a week, upgrading to Ultra 5x or 20x eliminates workflow stalls.

How do .antigravityignore and /usage cut wasted quota by 30%–50%?

Adding a .antigravityignore file at your repo root to exclude node_modules/, dist/, .next/, .astro/, *.lock, and binary assets prevents subagents from burning hundreds of thousands of tokens indexing build artifacts. Run /usage (or /quota) anytime to monitor your active 5-hour bucket.

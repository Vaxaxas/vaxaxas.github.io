What Is an Antigravity Top-Up Service?

Antigravity is built around autonomous coding agents, which quickly exhaust standard Pro limits during multi-file refactors. Google AI Ultra unlocks 5x to 20x higher capacity, yet developers worldwide often hit two roadblocks:

  • Payment & Region Friction: Local debit/credit cards get declined or regions aren’t supported (see our card-decline troubleshooting guide).
